Maasai Mara National Reserve

Witness the great wildebeest migration, see the Big Five, and experience the rich Maasai culture in one of Africa's most famous wildlife reserves.

Home to lions, elephants, cheetahs, and countless other species, the Mara offers unparalleled game viewing opportunities.

Nakuru National Park

Famous for its flamingo-lined lake and both black and white rhinos, Lake Nakuru National Park is a birdwatcher's paradise and a sanctuary for endangered species.

Enjoy stunning views of the lake surrounded by wooded and bushy grasslands.

Flamingoes of Lake Nakuru

Witness the spectacular pink flocks that make Lake Nakuru world-famous

The Pink Phenomenon

Lake Nakuru is renowned for its enormous flocks of lesser flamingos that often number in the millions, creating a breathtaking pink shoreline that can be seen from space.

These beautiful birds feed on algae in the lake's alkaline waters, creating one of Africa's most iconic wildlife spectacles.

In addition to flamingos, Lake Nakuru National Park hosts over 400 other bird species, making it a true paradise for birdwatchers and photographers.
