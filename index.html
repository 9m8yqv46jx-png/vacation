Explore Our Safari Destinations

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Witness the great wildebeest migration, see the Big Five (lion, elephant, leopard, rhino, buffalo), and experience the rich Maasai culture in one of Africa's most famous wildlife reserves.

Home to lions, elephants, cheetahs, and countless other species, the Mara offers unparalleled game viewing opportunities.

Nakuru National Park

Famous for its flamingo-lined lake and both black and white rhinos, Lake Nakuru National Park is a birdwatcher's paradise and a sanctuary for endangered species.

Enjoy stunning views of the lake surrounded by wooded and bushy grasslands, home to over 450 bird species.
